Internet

|ˈin-tər-ˌnet|
noun (the Internet)

an electronic communications network that connects computer networks and organizational computer facilities around the world: the guide is also available on the Internet | [as modifier]: Internet access | an Internet access

Origin

1970s (denoting a computer network connecting two or more smaller networks): from inter- 'reciprocal, mutual' + network
